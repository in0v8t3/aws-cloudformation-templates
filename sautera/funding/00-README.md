## Convention

Anything written as `[FILL: ...]` is a fact only you have. These are deliberately
left blank rather than estimated — every one of them is a representation to a
third party, and a wrong number in a funding application is worse than a gap.

**Current decision (2026-08-24):** no venture raise, so no US entity — register
in Grenada only. See `01` §5–6 for the reasoning and `04` for what could not be
verified about app store eligibility.

Nothing in these documents should be submitted until the entity question in
`01` is settled.
