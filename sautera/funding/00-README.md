## Convention

Anything written as `[FILL: ...]` is a fact only you have. These are deliberately
left blank rather than estimated — every one of them is a representation to a
third party, and a wrong number in a funding application is worse than a gap.

**Current decision (2026-08-24):** no venture raise; register in Grenada only,
owned directly, with "Sendall Holding" as a brand umbrella rather than an
ownership chain.

**Blocking item:** the owner is a US citizen, so US reporting follows the person
regardless of jurisdiction. Which Grenadian *entity form* is registered
determines whether the US classification choice survives at all, and it cannot be
undone. Read `05` §3.1 before anything is filed in Grenada.

Nothing in these documents should be submitted until the entity question in
`01` is settled.
