## Convention

Anything written as `[FILL: ...]` is a fact only you have. These are deliberately
left blank rather than estimated — every one of them is a representation to a
third party, and a wrong number in a funding application is worse than a gap.

**Current decision (2026-08-24).** The owner is a dual US/Grenadian citizen.
Register a Grenadian private limited company, own it directly, elect disregarded
treatment for US tax on Form 8832. Nothing else. See `06`.

**Read `06` first.** Files `01`–`05` record how the analysis got there, including
two arguments that were withdrawn along the way; they are kept because knowing
what was ruled out and why is part of the decision record.

**Two blocking items,** both in `06` §8: which Grenadian entity form preserves
the check-the-box option (specialist, before registration), and whether Grenada
appears in Apple's and Google's enrollment country selectors (ten minutes, free).
Neither blocks starting the website, which is step one.

Nothing in these documents should be submitted until the entity question in
`01` is settled.
